Ergo-S Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

Introduction

Ergo-S is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles your information.

Information Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

Ergo-S operates entirely on your device. Camera data is processed locally using Apple Vision framework. Assessment data and videos are stored locally only.

Data Storage

All data remains on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Third-Party Services

Ergo-S does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services.

Contact

For questions: support@ergos-app.com
