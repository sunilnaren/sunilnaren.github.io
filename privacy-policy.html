In short: ErgoSafe does not collect any data. Everything stays on your device, nothing is uploaded, there are no accounts, and there is no analytics, advertising or third-party tracking code in the app.

Introduction

ErgoSafe ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit or receive any of your information. The app has no network code and no server. The following data is created and kept on your device only:

  • Camera data. The app accesses your device's camera to perform ergonomic assessments. Video frames are processed locally using Apple's Vision framework. Video is never transmitted anywhere.
  • Assessment data. Results, including RULA scores and per-region risk analysis, are stored locally on your device.
  • Recorded videos. If you choose to save a recording, it is stored locally in the app's own storage.
  • Preferences. Unit and app-lock settings are stored locally.

How information is used

  • To perform ergonomic risk assessments.
  • To store and display your assessment history.
  • To generate corrective actions, exports and reports that you request.

Storage and security

All data is stored locally on your device and encrypted at rest using iOS data protection. We have no access to your personal data, videos or assessment results. Your data is further protected by your device's built-in security features, including device encryption and, if you enable it, Face ID or Touch ID app lock.

Recording other people

Because assessments involve filming a person at work, the app requires you to acknowledge a consent notice before the camera opens. You are responsible for obtaining the consent of anyone you record and for complying with the workplace and privacy laws that apply to you.

Third-party services

This app does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services. All processing occurs on device using Apple's native frameworks.

Your rights and choices

  • Delete individual assessments, projects or recordings at any time in the app.
  • Delete everything at once with Settings → Delete All Data.
  • Revoke camera access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data.

Because none of your data ever reaches us, there is nothing for us to retrieve, correct or delete on your behalf.

Children's privacy

This app is a professional workplace tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
