ErgoSafe turns a short video of someone working into a RULA ergonomic risk score, then helps you track the fix through to a verified re-assessment.

What it does

  • Scores neck, trunk, upper arm, lower arm, wrist and legs against the published RULA worksheet.
  • Reports how reliable each reading is: joint detection quality, joints that were not visible, and whether the camera angle suited the measurement.
  • Scores the worst sustained posture across the whole recording, not a single frame.
  • Raises corrective actions with an owner, due date and status, and links a later re-assessment to measure whether risk actually fell.
  • Exports to CSV or JSON, and generates per-project PDF reports.

Privacy

All analysis runs on your device using Apple's Vision framework. Nothing is uploaded, there are no accounts, and there is no analytics or advertising code. Assessments and recordings are encrypted at rest. See the Privacy Policy.

Medical disclaimer. ErgoSafe is a workplace screening tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide a diagnosis or treatment advice. Consult a doctor about any pain, injury or symptoms, and before making any decisions about your health. Always confirm findings with a qualified ergonomist.
