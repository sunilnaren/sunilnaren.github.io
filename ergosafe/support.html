Getting started

  1. Open ErgoSafe and tap New Assessment.
  2. Acknowledge the consent notice. Because the app records other people, this appears before the camera opens for the first time.
  3. Tap Open Camera and allow camera access when iOS asks.
  4. Point the camera at the worker. Filming from the side gives the most accurate scores.
  5. Tap the red button to record 10–30 seconds, then tap it again to stop.
  6. The results screen shows the risk score, how confident the reading is, and a breakdown by body region.

Common questions

Why does my score seem too high or too low?

Single-camera pose estimation cannot observe wrist twist, shoulder elevation or arm abduction, so these are scored as neutral and risk can be understated. Filming from the side rather than head-on improves trunk and neck accuracy. The results screen reports the capture quality and lists any joints that were not visible — check that panel first.

What does the confidence figure mean?

It combines how reliably joints were detected, how many were visible, how far away the subject was, and whether the camera angle suited the measurement. A low confidence reading is a prompt to re-record, not a result to act on.

Is this a NIOSH lifting assessment?

No. Lift frequency, duration, horizontal reach, asymmetry and grip coupling are not captured, so results are not a NIOSH Lifting Equation outcome. ErgoSafe implements RULA (McAtamney & Corlett, 1993).

I denied camera access. How do I turn it back on?

Open the iOS Settings app, then Privacy & Security → Camera, and switch ErgoSafe on.

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your device, encrypted at rest. Nothing is uploaded and there are no accounts.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings inside the app and choose Delete All Data. This permanently removes every assessment, project, corrective action and recording. Deleting the app also removes all stored data.

How do I get the results out?

Go to Reports. You can export assessments and corrective actions as CSV or JSON, or generate a per-project PDF report.
