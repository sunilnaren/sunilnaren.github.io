Ergo-S

AI-Powered Ergonomic Risk Assessment

Ergo-S uses advanced computer vision and the RULA (Rapid Upper Limb Assessment) methodology to analyze workplace postures and identify musculoskeletal disorder (MSD) risks in real-time.

📹
Real-Time Analysis

Point your camera at a worker and get instant RULA scores and risk assessments.

📊
Comprehensive Reports

Generate PDF reports with detailed body part risk breakdowns and recommendations.

📁
Project Organization

Organize assessments into projects for different workstations, clients, or audits.

🔒
Privacy First

All processing happens on-device. No data is sent to external servers.

Medical Disclaimer: Ergo-S is designed as a screening and educational tool for ergonomic assessment. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. The RULA scores and risk assessments provided are estimates based on pose detection and should not replace professional ergonomic evaluations or medical advice. Always consult with qualified healthcare professionals and certified ergonomists before making decisions about workplace modifications or treatment. Users should seek a doctor's advice before making any medical decisions based on information from this app.
