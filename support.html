How to Use Ergo-S

  1. Start an Assessment - Tap the "Start Assessment" button on the main screen
  2. Position Your Camera - Point your device at a person working at their desk or workstation
  3. Record - Capture 10-30 seconds of video for best results
  4. Review Results - Get instant RULA (Rapid Upper Limb Assessment) risk scores and recommendations

Frequently Asked Questions

What is RULA?

RULA (Rapid Upper Limb Assessment) is a validated ergonomic assessment method used to evaluate posture-related injury risk. Scores range from 1 (low risk) to 7 (high risk requiring immediate action).

Is my data private?

Yes. All video processing happens entirely on your device using Apple's Vision framework. No data is ever sent to external servers. See our Privacy Policy for details.

What devices are supported?

Ergo-S requires iOS 17.0 or later and works on iPhone and iPad.

How accurate is the assessment?

Ergo-S uses Apple's advanced pose detection technology for body tracking. For best results, ensure good lighting and a clear view of the person being assessed.

Can I delete my assessment history?

Yes. You can delete individual assessments from the app, or uninstall the app to remove all data.

Troubleshooting

  • Camera not working? - Make sure you've granted camera permissions in Settings > Ergo-S > Camera
  • No pose detected? - Ensure the full upper body is visible and there's adequate lighting
  • App crashes? - Try restarting the app or your device. If issues persist, contact support.
